TRUST & SAFETY

What Lockily means by a “Lock”

✓

A Lock is a claim

It records that an account holder says they are in a current romantic relationship with a specific phone number. Verification is shown separately only when the stated verification step has been completed.

≠

A Lockily check is not proof of cheating

Multiple claims can conflict for many reasons. Lockily reports relationship signals and activity; it does not declare guilt.

⌁

Other claimants stay private

No names, phone numbers, chats, photos or identities of another claimant are shown to you.

♥

Multiple Locks allowed

Talking stages, situationships, dating, engagements and marriages can all be locked. Claims must remain truthful, personal and lawful.
